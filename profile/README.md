## About

VIGIL-IoT is an engineering and research initiative focused on understanding and improving the security of embedded and Internet of Things (IoT) systems.

Connected systems span physical devices, firmware, communication protocols, applications, networks, and services. A weakness in one layer can affect the others, so VIGIL-IoT looks at the system as a whole and explores practical, evidence-led ways to strengthen it.

## Areas of exploration

- **Embedded and device security** — hardware trust boundaries, interfaces, and device configuration.
- **Firmware security** — boot and update integrity, exposed functionality, secrets, and dependencies.
- **IoT communication and trust** — device identity, authentication, message integrity, protocols, and trust relationships.
- **Network resilience** — exposure, visibility, segmentation, and containment.
- **Defensive intelligence** — detection and mitigation approaches suited to resource-constrained systems, evaluated against evidence.

## How we work

**Observe → Analyze → Validate → Harden**

We map system behavior and trust relationships, examine assumptions and exposed surfaces, validate security hypotheses through controlled and repeatable experiments, and evaluate defensive changes.

## Principles

- **Evidence first:** make security claims that can be observed and reviewed.
- **Think in layers:** consider how hardware, firmware, communication, networks, and applications interact.
- **Respect constraints:** account for the memory, compute, bandwidth, and energy limits of embedded devices.
- **Test responsibly:** work with owned devices, simulations, isolated lab networks, or explicit authorization.
- **Be transparent:** state assumptions, capabilities, and limitations clearly.
